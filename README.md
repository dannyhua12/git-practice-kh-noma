Article Reflection: AI and Software Development
Article

The Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-effects-of-generative-ai-on-high-skilled-work-evidence-from-three-field-experiments-with-software-developers/

Authors: Zheyuan (Kevin) Cui, Mert Demirer, Sonia Jaffe, Leon Musolff, Sida Peng, and Tobias Salz
Published: Management Science, 2026

I find this article interesting because it looks at generative AI in software development as a tool that can fundamentally change how people work. The researchers used and analyzed randomized field experiments at Microsoft, Accenture, and another large company, combining data from 4,867 software developers. Their analysis found that developers with AI coding assistants completed more tasks, although the results varied across the individual experiments and the measured effect was larger for less-experienced developers.
So what happens to human work when AI becomes part of it? I have experience using AI in software development myself in personal projects, but this article makes me more interested in studying the human side of these tools. If AI can change how developers complete tasks, it may also change how they learn, solve problems, collaborate, and decide what parts of software development require their attention. This connects to my interest in computational social science because I want to understand technology as something that changes human behavior and social systems.

Source
Cui, Z. K., Demirer, M., Jaffe, S., Musolff, L., Peng, S., & Salz, T. (2026). The Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers. Management Science. https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2025.00535
